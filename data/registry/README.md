# 🇮🇳 Sarathi AI — India Data Registry

**Developer:** David | **Org:** Nexuzy Lab, Kolkata

This registry maps all official and public India data sources used in Sarathi AI across 13 categories.

## Categories

| # | Category | Sources | Use in Sarathi |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Government | OGD India, NDAP, MoSPI | Factual answering, filters, search |
| 2 | Tourism | Ministry of Tourism, WB Tourism, Kolkata hotels | Travel guidance, hotel recommendations |
| 3 | Weather | IMD, NCPOR, MOSDAC | Weather queries, seasonal travel advice |
| 4 | Police | BPR&D, NCRB, Digital Police | Crime stats, police org info |
| 5 | Election | CSEP, Lokniti, Datameet, ECI | Politics and election queries |
| 6 | Economy | RBI, MoSPI, World Bank India | Finance and economy answers |
| 7 | Sports | Cricsheet, IPL Kaggle, Olympics | Sports queries |
| 8 | Maps | OpenStreetMap, GADM, Datameet Maps | Route planning, location lookup |
| 9 | Cyber | CERT-In, NCIIPC, AIKosh | Cybersecurity awareness answers |
| 10 | History | National Archives, Gutenberg, Archive.org | Historical queries |
| 11 | Social | Reddit India (public), GDELT News | Trending topics, news |
| 12 | Religion | Gita, Quran, Ramayana (Gutenberg) | Religious queries |
| 13 | Legal | IPC PDF, India Code | Law queries |

## Note
Sarathi AI uses only **public, licensed, aggregate, and official** datasets.
No private personal data, face data, or identity-level records are collected.
